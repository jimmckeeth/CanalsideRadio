# Privacy Policy — Canalside Radio

*Last updated: June 2026*

Canalside Radio ("the app") is a live radio streaming app for The Canalside Radio. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use it.

## Data we collect

The app collects no personally identifiable information. Specifically:

- No account registration or login is required
- No name, email address, or contact information is collected
- No location data is accessed or stored
- No usage analytics or crash reporting is sent by the app itself

## Preferences stored on your device

The app stores your chosen app theme and preferred audio stream locally on your device using Android/iOS standard storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device.

## Network activity

The app connects to The Canalside Radio stream servers solely to deliver the audio stream to you. Standard network request metadata (IP address, device type) may be visible to the stream server operator as part of normal internet communication.

## Third-party services

The app uses [Shorebird](https://shorebird.dev) to deliver over-the-air updates. Shorebird may collect minimal, non-identifiable technical data (app version, update status) to operate this service. See [Shorebird's privacy policy](https://shorebird.dev/privacy) for details.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
